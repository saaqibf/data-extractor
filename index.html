  • 🧹 Clean up trims spaces and drops blank or duplicate rows. 🔁 Find & replace fixes a spelling everywhere at once.
  • 🛠️ Column tools splits "Full Name" into first and last, combines them back, fixes ALL CAPS names, and removes duplicate rows by email.
  • 📊 Column summary gives the total, average, smallest and largest of every number column, and flags columns with gaps.
  • 🔎 Find specific things pulls out every email, phone number, link, dollar amount or date, or searches for any word. 👤 Contact list turns a messy email thread into a Name + Email table ready for a mail merge.
  • Click a heading to sort, double-click it to rename. Click any cell to edit it, or the ✕ on a row to delete it.
Everything you extract stays in the Viewing dropdown, so nothing is ever thrown away. Close the tab by accident and it offers your work back next time.

Paste any web address and press Extract. The tool tries three different ways to read the page, so most sites work. If a page has tables, you get the tables. If it does not, you get the readable text, and it is loaded into the 🔎 Find tab automatically so you can pull out emails, links, amounts or any word you want. If a site blocks every route, copy the part you want and use the Paste anything tab.

Drop files here, or anywhere on the page. PDF: reads the text and rebuilds rows and columns, stitching pages that share the same columns and merging across several files. Excel: opens every sheet, formulas come through as their answers. Word: pulls out any tables, plus the text. Also takes .csv and .txt. The only thing that will not work is a scanned photo of a page, because there is no text in it to read.
