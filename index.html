How to use this (3 steps)
  1. Get your data in: drop a PDF on the "From a PDF" tab, or copy a table from any website or Excel and paste it in "Paste anything." (The URL tab works on some sites; if a site blocks it, the paste tab always works.)
  2. Check the table that appears below.
  3. Click "Download CSV" and open it in Excel or Google Sheets. Done.
Not sure? Click "Show me an example" in the Paste anything tab and watch it work. Nothing you put here leaves your computer.
